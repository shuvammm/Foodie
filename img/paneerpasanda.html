Paneer Pasanda

Ingredients
Here are the ingredients you'll need to make paneer pasanda:
the Paneer Filling (optional):
Paneer: 250 grams, crumbled
Khoya (optional): 3 tablespoons, crumbled (can be substituted with grated cheese)
Potato: 2 small, boiled and mashed
Almonds: ¼ cup, finely chopped
Cashews: ¼ cup, finely chopped
Raisins: 2 tablespoons, chopped (optional)
Ginger: 1 tablespoon, chopped
Green chili: 1, chopped (adjust for spice preference)
Salt: ½ teaspoon
Red chili powder: ½ teaspoon
Cardamom & Mace powder: ½ teaspoon
Mint leaves: 1 tablespoon, chopped
Coriander leaves: 1 tablespoon, chopped
For the Paneer Pasanda Gravy:
Oil: 1 tablespoon
Ghee (optional): 1 tablespoon
Ginger garlic paste: 1 tablespoon
Curd: 3 tablespoons
Turmeric powder: 1 teaspoon
Red chili powder: 1 teaspoon
Coriander powder: 1 teaspoon
Salt: 1 teaspoon
Khoya (optional): 1 tablespoon, crumbled
Cashew paste: ¼ cup (made from pre-soaked cashews)
Cardamom & Mace powder: ½ teaspoon
Fresh cream (optional): ¼ cup
Garam masala: 1 teaspoon
Fresh coriander leaves: Chopped, for garnish

Instructions
1: Making the Paneer Filling (Optional)


1.Prepare the Filling Ingredients:
Crumble the paneer and khoya (if using).
Boil and mash the potatoes.
Finely chop the almonds, cashews, raisins (if using), ginger, green chili, mint leaves, and coriander leaves.
Mix the Filling:
In a bowl, combine crumbled paneer, mashed potato, chopped nuts, ginger, green chili, salt, red chili powder, cardamom & mace powder, chopped mint, and coriander leaves. Mix well to form a stuffing.
Part 2: Preparing the Paneer
Cut the Paneer: Slice the paneer block into rectangular pieces.
Prepare the Paneer Pockets (Optional):
If using the filling, take a paneer slice and place a generous amount of the filling mixture on top. Cover with another paneer slice to create a pocket. Gently press the edges to seal.
Part 3: Making the Gravy Heat the Fat: Heat oil and ghee (if using) in a pan over medium heat.
Sauté the Aromatics: Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook until fragrant.
Cook the Yogurt Base: Add the yogurt and cook until it releases oil from the sides.
Add Dry Spices: Stir in turmeric powder, red chili powder, coriander powder, and salt. Mix well.
Enrich the Gravy (Optional): Add crumbled khoya (if using) and cook for a minute.
Grind the Cashews: Grind the soaked cashews into a smooth paste with a little water.
Add the Cashew Paste and Water: Pour the cashew paste into the pan and simmer for 3-4 minutes. Add a little water to adjust the gravy consistency.
Finishing Touches: Sprinkle garam masala and stir well. Add fresh cream (optional) for extra richness.
Part 4: Cooking the Paneer
Shallow Fry the Paneer (Optional): If using paneer pockets, heat oil in a separate pan and shallow fry them until golden brown. If using plain paneer cubes, simply add them to the simmering gravy.
Simmer and Flavor: Once the paneer is cooked (fried or added to gravy), simmer for a few minutes to allow the flavors to meld.
Garnish and Serve: Turn off the heat. Garnish with chopped fresh coriander leaves and serve hot with rice, naan, or roti.
