Paneer Korma

Ingredients
For the paneer (Indian cottage cheese):
Milk - full-fat milk works best for paneer
Lemon juice - fresh lemon juice will give the paneer a tangy flavor
For the korma gravy:
Oil - any neutral oil, like canola or vegetable oil, can be used
Ghee (clarified butter) - for a richer flavor (optional)
Bay leaf
Cinnamon stick
Star anise (optional)
Cardamoms
Cloves
Shahi jeera (caraway seeds) - or regular cumin seeds
Ginger-garlic paste
Green chili (optional) - for a touch of heat
Onions - thinly sliced
Tomatoes - chopped
Turmeric powder
Coriander powder
Red chili powder
Garam masala
Water or coconut milk
Salt to taste
Fresh coriander leaves - for garnish
Paneer cubes - fried or pan-fried until golden brown

Paneer Korma

Instructions
1.Make the Korma Paste: Grind together blanched almonds/cashews, onions, tomatoes, ginger-garlic paste, and some water into a smooth paste.

2. Sauté the Spices: Heat oil in a pan, add whole spices like bay leaf, cloves, cardamom, cinnamon, and cumin. Once fragrant, add the ginger-garlic paste and saute till aromatic.

3. Cook the Korma Base: Add chopped onions and fry till translucent. Add the korma paste and cook till the oil separates. Add turmeric, red chili powder, coriander powder, and garam masala. Saute for a couple of minutes.

4. Simmer the Gravy: Add water or diluted coconut milk (optional) and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer till the gravy thickens.

5. Add Paneer: Gently fold in cubed paneer and simmer for a few minutes to allow it to absorb flavors.

Finishing Touches: Turn off the heat. Add cream (optional) and garnish with chopped cilantro.
