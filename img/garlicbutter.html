Garlic Butter Chicken

Ingredients
Here are the ingredients you'll need to make garlic butter chicken:
Main Ingredients:
Chicken: Boneless, skinless chicken breasts or thighs are common choices. You'll need about 1-1/2 to 2 pounds, depending on the number of servings.
For Seasoning:
Olive Oil: 1-2 tablespoons for searing the chicken.
Salt & Pepper: To taste.
Optional Seasonings: You can add other seasonings like paprika, garlic powder, or Italian seasoning for extra flavor.
For the Garlic Butter Sauce:
Butter: 3-4 tablespoons unsalted butter.
Garlic: 2-3 cloves, minced.
Fresh Parsley (Optional): Chopped parsley adds a fresh touch to the sauce.
Optional Ingredients:
Chicken Broth or White Wine: ½ cup, for added moisture and flavor to the pan sauce.
Lemon Juice: A squeeze of lemon juice can brighten up the flavors.
Sides (Optional):
Mashed potatoes, rice, or pasta are all delicious accompaniments to garlic butter chicken.

Garlic Butter Chicken

Instructions
Garlic butter chicken is a simple yet flavorful dish perfect for a quick and satisfying meal. Here's a breakdown of the process:

Preparation:
Prep the Chicken: Pat the chicken breasts or thighs dry with paper towels. This helps achieve crispier skin. Season the Chicken: Season both sides of the chicken with salt, pepper, and any other spices you prefer (paprika, garlic powder, Italian seasoning).
Cooking the Chicken:
Heat the Oil: In a large skillet over medium heat, add olive oil.
Sear the Chicken: Carefully place the chicken pieces in the hot oil and sear for 3-4 minutes per side, or until golden brown. Don't overcrowd the pan, cook in batches if needed.
Optional Deglazing (Adding Moisture): Once the chicken is seared, you can add a splash of chicken broth, white wine, or water to the pan. Scrape up any browned bits stuck to the bottom with a wooden spoon to deglaze the pan and add flavor to the sauce.
Making the Garlic Butter Sauce:
Reduce Heat: Lower the heat to medium-low.
Add Butter and Garlic: Add the butter to the pan and let it melt. Once melted, add the minced garlic and cook for about 30 seconds, until fragrant. Be careful not to burn the garlic.
Finish the Sauce (Optional): Add chopped fresh parsley (if using) and a squeeze of lemon juice (if using) for extra flavor.
immering and Finishing:
Simmer the Chicken: Spoon the garlic butter sauce over the chicken and simmer for 3-5 minutes, or until the chicken is cooked through (165°F internal temperature).
Baste the Chicken (Optional): While simmering, baste the chicken occasionally with the pan juices for extra flavor.
Serving:
Transfer the Chicken: Once cooked, transfer the chicken to a plate and cover loosely with foil to keep warm.
Spoon the Sauce: Spoon the remaining garlic butter sauce from the pan over the chicken.
Serve: Serve the garlic butter chicken with your favorite sides like mashed potatoes, rice, or pasta.
